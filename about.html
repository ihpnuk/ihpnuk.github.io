About IHPN-UK

Who we are

The Igbo Healthcare Professionals Network UK (IHPN-UK, also written IHPNUK) is a professional community advancing excellence, leadership and mentorship among Igbo healthcare professionals in the United Kingdom.

Vision

Where we are going

To be the leading network of Igbo healthcare professionals in the United Kingdom, advancing professional excellence, leadership, mentorship, community health improvement, and healthcare collaboration between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Our mission

  • Promote professional development and career advancement.
  • Provide mentorship and support to newly qualified and early-career professionals.
  • Facilitate networking and collaboration across healthcare disciplines.
  • Support leadership development and representation within healthcare.
  • Improve health outcomes within communities.
  • Strengthen healthcare partnerships between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.
  • Promote research, innovation and knowledge exchange.

Governance

How we are run

IHPN-UK was founded by its Founding President, who provides strategic leadership and sets the direction and priorities of the network.

We are setting up an Executive Committee, to be appointed by the Founder and President, to help deliver our programmes, events and membership growth. We aim to hold at least one Annual Meeting each year to review achievements and plan ahead, in person or online.

Planned Executive Committee roles

  • Founder and President
  • Vice President

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is IHPN-UK?

IHPN-UK (also written IHPN UK or IHPNUK) is the Igbo Healthcare Professionals Network UK. We are a professional network for Igbo healthcare professionals who live in the United Kingdom. People sometimes call us the Igbo healthcare network, the Igbo health network or simply IHPN.

Is IHPN-UK the same as the Independent Healthcare Providers Network?

No. The Independent Healthcare Providers Network also uses the initials IHPN, but it is a separate organisation and we have no connection with it. If you are looking for the network of Igbo healthcare professionals in the UK, you are in the right place.

Is there a network for Igbo doctors, nurses and other healthcare professionals in the UK?

Yes, that is what we are. IHPN-UK brings together Igbo doctors, nurses, midwives, pharmacists, optometrists, psychologists, health visitors, public health practitioners, healthcare assistants, support workers, managers and researchers across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Who can join?

Anyone working in healthcare who lives in the UK and has at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity. Every career stage is welcome, from newly qualified staff to senior leaders and retired professionals. We are not accepting healthcare students at present.

How do I join?

Apply online in about 3 minutes, send us a direct message on TikTok (@ihpnuk), or email ihpnuk@outlook.com.
