Black History Month look-back • 1 November 2026

Sickle cell and the Ro blood type

Black History Month has ended, but showing up for each other hasn’t. Here’s why Black heritage blood donors matter so much, and how to check if you can give.

Nearly 1 in 2 Black heritage donors have Ro

Ro is a blood subtype that’s rare in the wider population. Many people with sickle cell need regular transfusions of closely matched blood, and Black heritage donors are the most likely to be a match.

150,000+babies born with sickle cell in Nigeria every year
≈19,000people living with sickle cell in the UK
Just 3%of blood donors are from Black communities

Most healthy adults can give. Each service has a quick eligibility check and online booking.
