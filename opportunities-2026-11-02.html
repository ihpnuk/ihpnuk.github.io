Psychology & social science academics

British Academy/Leverhulme Senior Research Fellowship

A year away from teaching and admin to finish a major piece of research.

  • Closes Wed 11 Nov, 5pm
  • Pays for your replacement, plus up to £5,000 costs
  • UK-wide
Who can apply and details
  • For staff employed by a UK university, another UK higher education institution or an Independent Research Organisation
  • Aimed at established researchers with published work of distinction; your age and job title are not taken into account
  • Any field in the humanities or social sciences. If your work is clinical or experimental psychology, check it fits with the British Academy first (grants@thebritishacademy.ac.uk)
  • The deadline is final: no late applications

The British Academy scheme page may still show last year’s round; these guidance notes are for the round that closes on 11 November 2026.

Senior nurses & midwives

Senior and Global Scholarship

An 18-month leadership scholarship from the Florence Nightingale Foundation.

  • Closes Sun 15 Nov, midnight
  • Fully funded; £1,500 employer contribution
  • UK-wide
Who can apply and details
  • Registered nurse or midwife with the NMC
  • Usually NHS Band 8d or above, or an equivalent role in the independent sector or military. Below 8d, you can apply if you hold a systems leadership role
  • You need support from an executive or board-level sponsor
  • Residentials include accommodation and meals; you pay your own travel to London
  • A different programme from last week’s Windrush Leadership Programme

Apply on the Florence Nightingale Foundation site → (opens in new tab)

Nurses, midwives & support workers in Scotland

RCN Scotland Nurse of the Year Awards 2027

Nominate a colleague whose work deserves recognition.

  • Nominations close Fri 20 Nov
  • Nominate online
  • Scotland
Who can apply and details
  • For nursing staff working in Scotland, in the NHS, care homes or the independent sector
  • 13 categories, including Adult Nursing, Mental Health Nursing, Community Nursing, Children’s Nursing and Midwifery, Clinical Leadership and Nursing Support Worker of the Year
  • Winners are announced at a ceremony in April 2027

Nominate on the RCN site → (opens in new tab)

Diabetes researchers

Diabetes UK Project Grants

Funding for a diabetes research project of up to five years.

  • Closes Tue 1 Dec
  • Up to £500,000
  • UK-wide
Who can apply and details
  • The lead applicant usually needs a tenured post, or must show they will be salaried for the whole grant
  • Research must take place at an established research institution in the UK; applicants must work at a UK academic institute or NHS Trust
  • One application per lead applicant in each round; no extensions to the deadline
  • Requests of £500,000 or more need a conversation with Diabetes UK first. Decisions in May 2027

See the scheme on Diabetes UK → (opens in new tab)

Dietitians

BDA Research Symposium 2026

A day of new dietetic research, open to the whole dietetic workforce.

  • Wed 2 Dec, 9.15am to 4.30pm
  • £78 BDA members, £110 non-members
  • Birmingham, in person
Who can apply and details
  • For all members of the dietetic workforce; non-dietitians are welcome too
  • Lower rates of £68 for BDA Dietetic Support Workforce members
  • Abstract submissions have closed, but registration is open

Newer consultants in Scotland, including O&G

NHS Research Scotland Clinician Researcher Fellowship

Two funded sessions a week of protected time for clinical research.

  • Closes Tue 8 Dec, midday
  • Salary for 2 sessions a week, up to 3 years
  • Scotland
Who can apply and details
  • For consultants with a CCT who have been substantive consultants for less than 6 years (whole-time equivalent) by March 2027
  • You must not already have research sessions paid for in another way
  • Covers NHS boards across Scotland through the four NRS regional nodes
  • Fellowships start on 1 April 2027. The CSO page also mentions other funding dates, so check with CSOfellowships@gov.scot if unsure

See the fellowship on the CSO site → (opens in new tab)

Researchers with a PhD in Wales

Health and Care Research Wales Advanced Fellowship

Up to three years full time (or six part time) to become an independent researcher.

  • Closes Wed 13 Jan 2027, 1pm
  • Up to £600,000
  • Wales
Who can apply and details
  • You need a research PhD (a submitted thesis is fine if it is awarded by interview)
  • You must be employed or based in Wales, or have a job offer from a Welsh host, for the whole fellowship
  • Open to clinical, health, social care and public health disciplines, including nurses, midwives, allied health professionals, pharmacists and psychologists
  • Not for people who already hold a professorship (honorary titles are fine). You need a named academic mentor
  • Fellowships start on 1 October 2027

See the scheme on the Health and Care Research Wales site → (opens in new tab)

GPs

Become a Fellow of the RCGP (FRCGP)

Recognition for GPs who have made a significant contribution, with no application fee.

  • Any time
  • No application fee
  • UK-wide
Who can apply and details
  • You need at least five continuous years as an RCGP Member, and, if clinically active in the UK, one revalidation cycle after your CCT
  • Show a significant contribution to community health, to the science or practice of medicine, or to general practice
  • Write a 500 to 1,000 word statement and name two supporters
  • Fellows are elected at Council four times a year

See how to apply on the RCGP site → (opens in new tab)
