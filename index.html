Building the next generation of healthcare leaders.

What we do

A professional home for Igbo healthcare talent

We connect people across disciplines and career stages so that knowledge, opportunity and support flow freely through our community. As a new network, founded in 2026, we are building these step by step.

Professional development

Talks, learning events and webinars to help members keep growing in their careers, with our first talk coming soon.

Mentorship

Coming soon: structured mentoring for newly qualified and early-career professionals, guided by those who have walked the path. Tell us on your application if you would like to mentor or be mentored.

Networking

Collaboration across healthcare disciplines, from the ward to the lab to the boardroom.

Leadership

Developing leaders and strengthening the representation of Igbo professionals within healthcare.

Community health

Health education and outreach initiatives that improve outcomes within our communities.

UK and Nigeria

Building healthcare partnerships, research and knowledge exchange between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Our vision
“To be the leading network of Igbo healthcare professionals in the United Kingdom, advancing professional excellence, leadership, mentorship, community health improvement, and healthcare collaboration between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.”

Who can join

Open to healthcare professionals of Igbo heritage

Membership is open to people working in healthcare who live in the UK and have at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity.

  • Doctors
  • Dentists
  • Nurses
  • Midwives
  • Pharmacists
  • Allied Health Professionals
  • Psychologists
  • Public Health Practitioners
  • Healthcare Scientists
  • Managers
  • Researchers
  • Academics
  • Entrepreneurs
