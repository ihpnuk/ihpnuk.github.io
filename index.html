Igbo Healthcare Professionals Network UK

Building the next generation of healthcare leaders.

IHPN-UK brings together Igbo doctors, nurses, midwives, pharmacists, allied health professionals, researchers, managers and students across the United Kingdom to grow, lead and give back.

What we do

A professional home for Igbo healthcare talent

We connect people across disciplines and career stages so that knowledge, opportunity and support flow freely through our community.

Professional development

Career advancement, learning events, webinars and conferences that keep members at the top of their field.

Mentorship

Structured mentoring for students and early-career professionals, guided by those who have walked the path.

Networking

Collaboration across healthcare disciplines, from the ward to the lab to the boardroom.

Leadership

Developing leaders and strengthening the representation of Igbo professionals within healthcare.

Community health

Health education and outreach initiatives that improve outcomes within our communities.

UK and Nigeria

Building healthcare partnerships, research and knowledge exchange between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.

Our vision
“To be the leading network of Igbo healthcare professionals in the United Kingdom, advancing professional excellence, leadership, mentorship, community health improvement, and healthcare collaboration between the United Kingdom and Nigeria.”

Who can join

Open to healthcare professionals and students of Igbo heritage

Membership is open to people working or studying in healthcare who live in the UK and have at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity.

  • Doctors
  • Dentists
  • Nurses
  • Midwives
  • Pharmacists
  • Allied Health Professionals
  • Psychologists
  • Public Health Practitioners
  • Healthcare Scientists
  • Managers
  • Researchers
  • Academics
  • Entrepreneurs
  • Students
