Almost every NHS role, from HCAs to consultants

NHS Clinical Entrepreneur Programme, Cohort 11

A free year of mentoring, workshops and events to turn your idea for better care into a real innovation.

  • Closes Fri 20 Nov 2026, 4pm: apply this week
  • Free to join (not a paid fellowship and no grants)
  • Open to NHS staff in England, Scotland and Wales
Who can apply and details
  • You need to be 18 or over and part of the NHS workforce. Roles listed include doctors and GPs, nurses, midwives, pharmacists, optometrists, dietitians and other allied health professionals, psychological professions, healthcare scientists, healthcare assistants and other clinical support staff, and healthcare managers. Public health is not named, so check with the team first
  • You need an innovation idea, clinical or non-clinical. It can be early stage if you have thought it through carefully
  • The application includes a one-minute video pitch and two letters of support from professional contacts, so start now. Write it yourself: the programme does not allow AI-written applications
  • Delivered by Anglia Ruskin University as an NHS England initiative. It runs for 12 months from March 2027, with CPD-accredited events, mentoring, workshops and an alumni network. You commit to at least half of the group events and four mentoring sessions

Public health specialty registrars

Faculty of Public Health Registrars Projects Scheme

Join a national Faculty project alongside your training: children’s health, AI and digital public health, and more.

  • Act Early project: apply by 1 Dec (negotiable)
  • Travel costs normally met by your deanery
  • Act Early is in Lambeth and Southwark, London; check each project’s location
Who can apply and details
  • For public health specialty registrars. Some projects ask for ST3 or above. These are projects with a named supervisor, not placements, so agree time with your training programme first
  • Act Early (children and young people): a six-month secondment, two days a week, in Lambeth and Southwark. The page gives 1 December 2026 as the start-date deadline, and says it can be negotiated
  • AI and Digital Public Health: a project planned for 2026 to 2027, with no deadline listed. Other projects on the page close sooner (one on 20 November), so check them today
  • Apply with the Faculty’s projects application form, as explained on the page

See the projects on the FPH site → (opens in new tab)

Scotland: nurses, AHPs, pharmacists, psychologists & public health

NHS Research Scotland Clinician Researcher Scheme

Two sessions a week of funded research time for three years, for NHS Scotland clinicians with no research time now.

  • Closes Tue 8 Dec 2026, midday
  • Salary costs for two sessions a week are funded
  • NHS Scotland only
Who can apply and details
  • For nurses, allied health professionals, pharmacists, biomedical and clinical scientists, public health specialists, psychologists and paramedics, registered with their professional body
  • You must be Band 6 or above, and not have been at Band 8c for more than six years by March 2027. You must not already have funded research sessions
  • You need a mentor and sign-off from your clinical director. Apply through the Chief Scientist Office’s online portal
  • Interviews are in February 2027 and fellowships start on 1 April 2027

See the scheme on the CSO site → (opens in new tab)

England: health and care professionals & researchers

NIHR Academy awards: doctoral, postdoctoral and professorships

Funded protected time to build a research career, at three levels: your first PhD, after your PhD, and professor.

  • First closes Wed 9 Dec (professorships); others 13 and 14 Jan
  • Funded: salary plus research and training costs
  • England only (Scotland and Wales are not taking part)
Who can apply and details
  • Doctoral Award (Cohort 4): for health and care professionals without a PhD (check the NIHR guidance for your profession). Three years of funded protected time, with salary, project and training costs (up to £500,000). Closes 1pm, Wed 13 January 2027
  • Postdoctoral Award (Cohort 4): for people with a relevant PhD or MD who do not hold a chair, including diabetes researchers. Two to five years with salary, a support post and research costs. Closes 1pm, Thu 14 January 2027
  • Research Professorships (Cohort 17): for senior health, public health and care researchers with an outstanding record. Up to £2 million over five years. You must be nominated by an English university or NHS body, and non-medical professions are prioritised. Closes 1pm, Wed 9 December 2026
  • Your employer or university must be in England for all three. Talk to your research office now, because each needs host sign-off

See the Doctoral Award on the NIHR site → (opens in new tab)

Nurses, midwives, pharmacists, dietitians, clinical psychologists & doctors

MRC Professional Doctorate Clinical Research Training Fellowship

Three years of salary and project costs to do a PhD while staying clinical, for registered healthcare professionals.

  • Closes Wed 13 Jan 2027, 4pm
  • Salary, project costs and home tuition fees for three years
  • Any UK nation, at an eligible UK research organisation
Who can apply and details
  • Open to healthcare professionals registered with a UK statutory regulator, including nurses, midwives, allied health professionals (such as dietitians), pharmacists, clinical psychologists and doctors
  • Doctors should be in specialty training and have completed their membership exams or equivalent. Non-medical professionals need to have finished professional training and hold a research master’s or equivalent. Clinical psychologists need their BPS-accredited professional doctorate before starting
  • Funds your salary and project costs for three years, with up to £25,000 a year for consumables, equipment and travel, plus tuition fees at the home rate
  • Apply through the UKRI Funding Service with your research office. Awards start between October 2027 and January 2028, so talk to a potential supervisor now

See the fellowship on the UKRI site → (opens in new tab)

Any health professional or researcher, any career stage

Academy of Medical Sciences Cross-sector Experience Awards

Up to £100,000 to spend 3 to 12 months working in industry, policy, a charity or another sector, then bring it back.

  • Closes Fri 12 Feb 2027, 4pm
  • Covers your base salary and on-costs, plus travel for you
  • Any UK nation (your employer must be UK-based)
Who can apply and details
  • Open to any discipline or sector, if the work links to improving human health. There is no set career stage and you do not need a PhD
  • You need a contract that runs beyond the end of the award, and a host organisation in a different sector (it can be outside the UK). You cannot have worked for the host before
  • The award pays your base salary, National Insurance and employer contributions. It does not cover indirect costs
  • Apply online through the Academy’s grant system

See the award on the Academy site → (opens in new tab)
