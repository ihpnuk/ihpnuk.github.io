Nurses, midwives, pharmacists, dietitians, clinical psychologists & doctors

MRC Professional Doctorate Clinical Research Training Fellowship

Three years of salary and project costs to do a PhD while staying clinical, for registered healthcare professionals.

  • Closes Wed 13 Jan 2027, 4pm
  • Salary, project costs and home tuition fees for three years
  • Any UK nation, at an eligible UK research organisation
Who can apply and details
  • Open to healthcare professionals registered with a UK statutory regulator, including nurses, midwives, allied health professionals (such as dietitians), pharmacists, clinical psychologists and doctors
  • Doctors should be in specialty training and have completed their membership exams or equivalent. Non-medical professionals need to have finished professional training and hold a research master’s or equivalent. Clinical psychologists need their BPS-accredited professional doctorate before starting
  • Funds your salary and project costs for three years, with up to £25,000 a year for consumables, equipment and travel, plus tuition fees at the home rate
  • Apply through the UKRI Funding Service with your research office. Awards start between October 2027 and January 2028, so talk to a potential supervisor now

Scotland: nurses, AHPs, pharmacists, psychologists & public health

NHS Research Scotland Clinician Researcher Scheme

Two sessions a week of funded research time for three years, for NHS Scotland clinicians with no research time now.

  • Closes Tue 8 Dec 2026, midday
  • Salary costs for two sessions a week are funded
  • NHS Scotland only
Who can apply and details
  • For nurses, allied health professionals, pharmacists, biomedical and clinical scientists, public health specialists, psychologists and paramedics, registered with their professional body
  • You must be Band 6 or above, and not have been at Band 8c for more than six years by March 2027. You must not already have funded research sessions
  • You need a mentor and sign-off from your clinical director. Apply through the Chief Scientist Office’s online portal
  • Interviews are in February 2027 and fellowships start on 1 April 2027

See the scheme on the CSO site → (opens in new tab)

Public health specialty registrars

Faculty of Public Health Registrars Projects Scheme

Join a national Faculty project alongside your training: children’s health, AI and digital public health, and more.

  • Act Early project: apply by 1 Dec (negotiable)
  • Travel costs normally met by your deanery
  • Act Early is in Lambeth and Southwark, London; check each project’s location
Who can apply and details
  • For public health specialty registrars. Some projects ask for ST3 or above. These are projects with a named supervisor, not placements, so agree time with your training programme first
  • Act Early (children and young people): a six-month secondment, two days a week, in Lambeth and Southwark. The page gives 1 December 2026 as the start-date deadline, and says it can be negotiated
  • AI and Digital Public Health: a project planned for 2026 to 2027, with no deadline listed. Other projects on the page close sooner (one on 20 November), so check them today
  • Apply with the Faculty’s projects application form, as explained on the page

See the projects on the FPH site → (opens in new tab)
