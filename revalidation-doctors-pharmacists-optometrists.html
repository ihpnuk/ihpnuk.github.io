Revalidation for doctors, pharmacists and optometrists

What the GMC, GPhC and GOC ask for, side by side. Save this page for your next cycle.

Revalidation at a glance

Each regulator does it differently. Here are the core requirements, checked against the regulators’ own websites.

5 yearsDoctors (GMC): usual revalidation cycle, with an appraisal every year
6 recordsPharmacists (GPhC): submitted every year when you renew
36 pointsOptometrists and dispensing opticians (GOC): by 31 December 2027

Slides: Revalidation for doctors, pharmacists and optometrists

Swipe or scroll sideways to see all 6 slides.

Doctors: GMC

You have an appraisal every year, based on Good medical practice and covering all of your work in the UK. Usually every five years, your responsible officer (or a GMC-approved suitable person) makes a recommendation to the GMC about your revalidation.

Bring six types of supporting information to your appraisals: continuing professional development, quality improvement activity, significant events, feedback from patients, feedback from colleagues, and compliments and complaints.

Pharmacists: GPhC

Every year, when you renew your registration, you submit six revalidation records: four CPD records, one peer discussion and one reflective account. If you have an annotation (for example, as an independent prescriber) or a specialist role, at least one of your six records must reflect on it.

Optometrists and dispensing opticians: GOC

The current CPD cycle runs from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2027. You need at least 36 CPD points, including at least 18 from interactive CPD, with at least one point in each of the four domains: professionalism, communication, clinical practice, and leadership and accountability. Aim for at least six points each year.

You also need to take part in at least one peer review, complete a reflective exercise with a peer and keep a personal development plan in your MyCPD account. Contact lens opticians and optometrists with a specialty registration have higher totals.
