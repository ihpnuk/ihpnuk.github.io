Opportunities • 26 October 2026

Opportunities this week

We check fellowships, courses and support so you don’t have to. This week there are six picks, so that nursing, midwifery, public health, optometry, pharmacy, support workers and researchers all get one.

Nurses & midwives • England

Windrush Leadership Programme

A leadership programme from the Florence Nightingale Foundation for registered nurses and midwives from Black, Asian and minority ethnic backgrounds, at least two years after qualifying, working in England for the NHS or an NHS-contracted provider. You lead an improvement project on health inequalities, with sessions online and a day at RADA in London.

Cost: places are funded by NHS England; your employer covers travel and accommodation. You need support from your line manager or Chief Nurse. Closes: Tuesday 8 December 2026. Cohorts start in April or June 2027.

Read the details and apply on the Florence Nightingale Foundation site →

Doctors and non-medical professionals • UK recruitment

Public health specialty training (ST1)

A route to becoming a public health consultant, open to doctors and to people from non-medical backgrounds. Good for anyone thinking of moving into population health.

Non-medical route needs: a completed 2:1 honours degree (ordinary degrees don’t count) or a master’s or higher, and at least 48 months’ whole-time-equivalent work experience by the closing date. That must include 24 months at Band 6 or equivalent in work relevant to population health, with at least 3 of those months between February 2024 and the closing date. Doctors apply through the medical route and need foundation competences. Cost: no application fee is listed; the assessment is at a test centre, so allow for travel. Closes: 4pm, Thursday 19 November 2026. Training starts August 2027. Posts are recruited across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Optometrists & opticians • online, UK-wide

The Habit Effect: AOP webinar

A one-hour interactive webinar on building healthier routines, for your patients and for your own wellbeing, run by Optometry Today with the AOP and sponsored by Alcon. Worth 1 interactive CPD point. To get the CPD point, don’t dial in by phone (audio only).

When: Tuesday 24 November 2026, 7pm to 8pm. Cost: free for AOP members, £25 for non-members.

Book on the AOP website →

Pharmacists • Great Britain (not Northern Ireland)

Pharmacist Support: free, confidential help

The pharmacy profession’s own charity offers free and confidential support, including Listening Friends peer support, counselling, financial help, debt and benefits advice, and wellbeing workshops. Pharmacists, trainees and their dependants can use it; pharmacy technicians can use the free website resources.

Cost: free. When: available now, no deadline.

Find support at pharmacistsupport.org →

Healthcare assistants & support workers • online, anywhere in the UK

Introducing practical healthcare (OpenLearn)

A free online course from The Open University, developed with the Social Partnership Network (which includes UNISON), for healthcare assistants and support staff who want to build their knowledge and move on in their careers. Earn digital badges as you go and a free Statement of Participation at the end.

Cost: free. When: online, no deadline. Open the course page, then follow its link to start.

Start the course on OpenLearn →

Researchers, academics & improvement leads • UK and Ireland

Join Q, the national improvement community

A free community for people working in, or interested in, improving health and care, who understand some structured approaches to improvement. Researchers, health professionals and patients are all welcome. It is hosted by the NHS Alliance and supported by the Health Foundation. Members get free events and webinars, networking, practical tools and chances to build improvement skills.

Cost: free. When: no deadline; apply any time.

Apply to join Q →
