Nurses & midwives

Windrush Leadership Programme

Funded leadership training for minority ethnic nurses and midwives.

  • Closes Tue 8 Dec
  • Funded by NHS England
  • England
Who can apply and details
  • Registered nurse or midwife (NMC) from a Black, Asian or minority ethnic background, at least two years after qualifying
  • Working in England for the NHS or an NHS-contracted provider
  • You lead an improvement project on health inequalities; sessions online plus a day at RADA in London
  • You need support from your line manager or Chief Nurse; your employer covers travel and accommodation
  • Cohorts start in April or June 2027

Apply on the Florence Nightingale Foundation site →

Doctors & non-medical professionals

Public health specialty training

Start training in August 2027 to become a public health consultant.

  • Closes Thu 19 Nov, 4pm
  • No application fee listed
  • England, Scotland, Wales, NI
Who can apply and details
  • Non-medical route: a completed 2:1 honours degree (ordinary degrees don’t count) or a master’s or higher
  • At least 48 months’ whole-time-equivalent work experience by the closing date, including 24 months at Band 6 or equivalent in work relevant to population health, with at least 3 of those months between February 2024 and the closing date
  • Doctors apply through the medical route and need foundation competences
  • The assessment is at a test centre, so allow for travel

Membership

Why join IHPN-UK?

Members hear about our talks, events and opportunities, including our mental health talk on Sunday 22 November. You can ask questions and get support in our members’ group, and connect with Igbo healthcare professionals across the UK.

Membership is open to healthcare professionals of Igbo heritage who live and work in the UK.
