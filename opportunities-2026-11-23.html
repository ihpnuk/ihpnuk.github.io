Nurses, midwives & health visitors

Burdett Trust Neighbourhood Health Community Engagement Awards

Up to £10,000 to lead a project that brings care closer to under-served or minoritised communities.

  • Closes Mon 14 Dec 2026, midday
  • Grants of up to £10,000
  • Any UK nation
Who can apply and details
  • The project lead must be an NMC-registered nurse or midwife; the grant guidance also names health visitors. Any seniority
  • Funds nurse-led, co-produced projects that engage under-served or minoritised communities. A “neighbourhood” can be a care home, faith group or school
  • You need a host organisation to hold the funds, plus support from your line manager and Director of Nursing
  • It does not fund research, professional education or conference attendance. Award holders join a community of practice

See the awards on the Burdett site → (opens in new tab)

Senior midwives

Royal College of Midwives Fellowship

The College’s highest honour, recognising an exceptional contribution to midwifery.

  • Apply by Sun 6 Dec 2026
  • No application fee listed; Fellows pay £5 a month on top of membership
  • Any UK nation (RCM members)
Who can apply and details
  • For NMC-registered midwives who have practised for more than seven years and have been RCM members for five continuous years
  • You need two pieces of evidence of an exceptional contribution to midwifery and three referees
  • Fellows can use the letters FRCM after their name
  • The RCM page gives both 6 and 7 December; apply by Sunday 6 December to be safe

See the Fellowship on the RCM site → (opens in new tab)

Pharmacy professionals who lead people or services

CPPE Leading for change, Cohort 11

A fully funded nine-month leadership programme for pharmacy professionals, built around a real improvement project at work.

  • Closes Tue 5 Jan 2027
  • Fully funded
  • CPPE is NHS England-funded; check eligibility if you work in Scotland or Wales
Who can apply and details
  • For any pharmacy professional with responsibility for leading people or services, in any sector
  • You need your line manager’s support and an agreed workplace mentor. Approval can take up to two weeks, so start now
  • Runs from January to September 2027: five online units, two full-day online workshops and a workplace quality improvement project
  • Not open to people on or already through CPGPE, MOCH or PCPEP

See the programme on the CPPE site → (opens in new tab)

Dietitians, including new researchers

BDA General and Education Trust research grants

Grants for dietetic research, with a welcome for new researchers and dietitians researching their own clinical practice.

  • Next deadline Wed 6 Jan 2027
  • Grants typically £5,000 to £40,000
  • Any UK nation
Who can apply and details
  • Open to anyone doing dietetic research. The Trust is particularly keen to support new researchers and dietitians researching their clinical practice
  • The 6 January deadline is for the February meeting; later deadlines are 7 April and 4 August 2027
  • It does not fund PhD fees, students, or computer equipment and software

See the grants on the BDA site → (opens in new tab)

Researchers with a doctorate, incl. psychologists & public health

UKRI Policy Fellowships 2027

A funded 18-month fellowship, including a year working inside government on real policy questions.

  • Closes Tue 16 Feb 2027, 4pm
  • Up to £180,000 (full economic cost)
  • UK, with host roles in Scotland, Wales and England
Who can apply and details
  • For early and mid-career researchers with a doctorate or equivalent research experience, employed at a UKRI-eligible organisation. Professors are not usually the target
  • Health-related host roles include Public Health Wales, the Department of Health and Social Care and the UK Health Security Agency, on topics such as prevention, gambling harms and genomics. Other hosts include the Scottish Government
  • Starts in October 2027 and includes a 12-month placement with the host. Your employer covers 20% of costs
  • Check the current dates on the UKRI Funding Service before you apply

See the fellowships on the UKRI site → (opens in new tab)

Pharmacists & pharmacy technicians

Pharmacy Research UK PhD Studentship 2027

Up to £100,000 for a full-time or part-time PhD in pharmacy practice research.

  • Closes Wed 24 Mar 2027, 1pm
  • Up to £100,000
  • Any UK nation
Who can apply and details
  • For UK-registered pharmacy professionals, including pharmacy technicians
  • Funds practice research only, not laboratory work
  • The 2027 round launched on 16 November 2026. Interviews are on 7 July 2027
  • You will need a university and supervisor, so start those conversations early

See the studentship on the PRUK site → (opens in new tab)
