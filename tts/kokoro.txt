Did you know? Nearly one in two Black heritage donors have a blood subtype called R O.
It's rare in the wider population, and many people with sickle cell need it.
Sickle cell is an inherited condition that affects red blood cells.
In Nigeria, at least a hundred and fifty thousand babies are born with it every year.
In the UK, about nineteen thousand people live with it. Yet just three per cent of blood donors are from Black communities.
