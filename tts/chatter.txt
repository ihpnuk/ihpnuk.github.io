Doctors, pharmacists and optometrists: due to revalidate? Here's exactly what your regulator asks for.
Doctors usually revalidate every five years. You need an annual appraisal, and your responsible officer makes your recommendation.
Bring six types of evidence: CPD, quality improvement, significant events, patient feedback, colleague feedback, and complaints and compliments.
Pharmacists submit six records every year: four CPD records, one peer discussion and one reflective account.
Optometrists need thirty-six CPD points by the end of twenty twenty-seven, including eighteen from interactive CPD, plus a peer review, a reflective exercise and a personal development plan.
