Membership

Join IHPN-UK

Become part of a growing network of Igbo healthcare professionals across the United Kingdom.

Eligibility

Who can join

Full membership is open to healthcare professionals, and to healthcare academics, managers, researchers and entrepreneurs, who:

  • live and work in the United Kingdom, and
  • have at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity.

Retired from healthcare? Retired professionals living in the UK cannot join as full members, but you may be invited to become an Honorary Member so the network can benefit from your experience. Email us to tell us about yourself.

Eligibility comes from your own heritage. It does not arise through marriage, civil partnership, relationship status or association with a member.

Eligible professions include

  • Doctors
  • Dentists
  • Nurses
  • Midwives
  • Pharmacists
  • Allied Health Professionals
  • Psychologists
  • Public Health Practitioners
  • Healthcare Scientists
  • Healthcare Managers
  • Healthcare Researchers
  • Healthcare Academics

This list is not exhaustive. If you work in healthcare and are unsure, get in touch. We are not accepting healthcare students at present.

How to apply

Joining is simple

Membership is by application and every application is reviewed.

  1. Fill in the form

    Our short online application form takes about three minutes. It asks for your name, email, profession, and where in the UK you are based.

  2. Application review

    Your application is reviewed by the Founder and President or a delegated member of the team. We may get in touch to confirm eligibility.

  3. Welcome to IHPN-UK

    Once approved, we will email you to welcome you into the network and invite you to our members’ groups.

Apply online Or message us on TikTok

Prefer email? Write to ihpnuk@outlook.com. Read how we look after your information in our privacy notice.

As a member you will

  • Uphold the values and objectives of the network.
  • Conduct yourself professionally and respect fellow members.
  • Contribute positively to our activities and reputation.
  • Follow the network’s policies and guidelines.

Our code of conduct

  • Treat others with dignity and respect.
  • No harassment, discrimination, bullying or abusive behaviour.
  • Maintain professional standards consistent with healthcare practice.
  • Avoid conduct that may bring the network into disrepute.

Honorary membership. The Founder and President may confer Honorary Membership on individuals who have made exceptional contributions to healthcare, education, leadership or the objectives of the network, including retired professionals who share their experience with members. Honorary Members may live in the UK without working here.
