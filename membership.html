Membership

Join IHPN-UK

Become part of a growing network of Igbo healthcare professionals and students across the United Kingdom.

Eligibility

Who can join

Membership is open to healthcare professionals, academics, managers, researchers, entrepreneurs and students who:

  • live in the United Kingdom, and
  • have at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity.

Eligibility comes from your own heritage. It does not arise through marriage, civil partnership, relationship status or association with a member.

Eligible professions include

  • Doctors
  • Dentists
  • Nurses
  • Midwives
  • Pharmacists
  • Allied Health Professionals
  • Psychologists
  • Public Health Practitioners
  • Healthcare Scientists
  • Healthcare Managers
  • Healthcare Researchers
  • Healthcare Academics
  • Healthcare Students

This list is not exhaustive. If you work in healthcare and are unsure, get in touch.

How to apply

Joining is simple

Membership is by application and every application is reviewed.

  1. Message us on TikTok

    Send a direct message to @ihpnuk saying you would like to join.

  2. Tell us about yourself

    Share your name, profession or course, and where in the UK you are based. We may ask for details to confirm eligibility.

  3. Application review

    Your application is reviewed by the Founder and President or a delegated member of the team.

  4. Welcome to IHPN

    Once approved, you will be welcomed into the network and our members’ community channels.

As a member you will

  • Uphold the values and objectives of the network.
  • Conduct yourself professionally and respect fellow members.
  • Contribute positively to our activities and reputation.
  • Follow the network’s policies and guidelines.

Our code of conduct

  • Treat others with dignity and respect.
  • No harassment, discrimination, bullying or abusive behaviour.
  • Maintain professional standards consistent with healthcare practice.
  • Avoid conduct that may bring the network into disrepute.

Honorary membership. The Founder and President may confer Honorary Membership on individuals who have made exceptional contributions to healthcare, education, leadership or the objectives of the network.
