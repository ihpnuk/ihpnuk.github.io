Obstetricians & gynaecologists

RCOG World Congress 2027

Three days of new evidence and CPD in obstetrics and gynaecology, with six months of recordings.

  • Early-bird rate ends Sat 9 Jan
  • Early bird US$950 for consultants (US$475 midwives and nurses)
  • Kuala Lumpur, 22 to 24 April 2027; open to all
Who can apply and details
  • Run by the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists with the Obstetrical and Gynaecological Society of Malaysia, at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre
  • 35 sessions across five streams, including eight plenaries
  • Registration includes the full programme, six months of on-demand recordings (subject to speaker permission), a certificate of attendance and CPD confirmation
  • Fees are in US dollars. From 10 January the standard rate is US$1,188 for consultants. Trainee and allied health professional rates are also listed
  • Abstract submission has closed, so this is a pick for attending, not presenting

See fees and register on the RCOG site → (opens in new tab)

Leaders and managers at any level

Developing your Confidence as a Leader: Team Dynamics

Two online mornings on how team dynamics shape behaviour, relationships and results.

  • Wed 20 and Thu 21 Jan, 9.30am to 1.30pm
  • No fee listed on the booking page
  • Online (MS Teams), from Scotland’s Leading to Change
Who can apply and details
  • Open to “leaders at all levels and from all roles”: you do not have to be a manager or lead a team to attend
  • Run by Leading to Change, the leadership programme for Scotland’s health, social care and social work system. If you work in England or Wales, check with the team that you can join
  • Register once and you get a Teams invitation for each morning. No booking deadline is shown, so book before 20 January
  • Covers the role of confidence in leadership, how expectations shape behaviour, and strategies to build your confidence

See the course and register → (opens in new tab)

Optometrists interested in research

College of Optometrists and Boots Opticians PhD Scholarship

A funded PhD in optometry or vision science, for a College member.

  • Closes Fri 19 Feb 2027
  • University fees plus a maintenance grant and research costs
  • At a UK university that teaches optometry
Who can apply and details
  • The candidate must be a member of The College of Optometrists
  • Prospective candidates or supervisors can apply. Supervisors need the approval of their Head of Department or School
  • The PhD must be at a UK university that offers an undergraduate optometry degree. Any optometry or vision science topic
  • One scholarship is available for 2026/27. The form, applicant information and terms are on the College page

See the scholarship on the College site → (opens in new tab)

Professors & senior social scientists, including psychology

ESRC Explore Awards

Large grants for ambitious, curiosity-driven social science research.

  • Open now; no deadline
  • £300,000 to £2 million (ESRC pays 80% of the full cost)
  • UK research organisations
Who can apply and details
  • Opened on 5 November 2026. No deadline: apply when ready. The Funding Service shows a placeholder closing date for system reasons
  • For novel, ambitious, curiosity-driven social science, with the potential for high reward but some uncertainty and risk. Social science must be more than half of the focus and effort; interdisciplinary work is welcome
  • Projects can last up to 60 months
  • Any career stage. You must be based at a UK research organisation eligible for ESRC funding. Teams have one project lead and up to three project co-leads

See the call on the UKRI site → (opens in new tab)
