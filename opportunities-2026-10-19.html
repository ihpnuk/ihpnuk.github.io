Opportunities this week

Every week we check fellowships, courses and events so our members don’t have to. Here are three this week that are open to everyone.

Churchill Fellowship

Fully funded 4 to 8 weeks of learning abroad on a health issue. For UK citizens living in the UK, from any profession. Now closed: applications closed on 20 October 2026. The next round opens in September 2027.

Sign up for alerts on churchillfellowship.org →

GMC Welcome to UK Practice

A free workshop (3 hours online or 5 hours in person) for doctors who qualified abroad and are new to UK practice.

Find out more on gmc-uk.org →

Edward Jenner Programme

A free online NHS leadership course with a Level 1 award, for people new to health and care or hoping to step into leadership.

Find out more from the NHS Leadership Academy →

Membership

IHPN-UK members get more

The full weekly roundup (three more picks this week, including our free mental health talk in late November), peer help with applications, jobs shared by members, and first places at IHPN-UK talks.

Membership is open to healthcare professionals of Igbo heritage living in the UK.
