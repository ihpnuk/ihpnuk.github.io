Know your rest rights at work

Work-life balance is one of the top topics for our members. Start with the legal minimums for breaks, rest between shifts and paid leave.

Your rest rights in numbers

These are the legal minimums for adult workers in Great Britain. Your contract, including NHS contracts, may give you more.

20 minbreak if you work more than 6 hours a day
11 hrsrest between working days
24 hrsoff each week, or 48 hours each fortnight
28 dayspaid leave a year on a 5-day week (5.6 weeks); your employer can include bank holidays

Working shifts or nights?

If your job needs round-the-clock cover, such as hospital work, you may not always get these breaks at the usual time. In that case you should get compensatory rest: the same amount of rest, taken later. The 20-minute break doesn’t have to be paid by law, so check your contract. Workers under 18 get more.
