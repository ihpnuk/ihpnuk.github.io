Your data

Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information when you apply to join IHPN-UK or get in touch.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

The Igbo Healthcare Professionals Network UK (IHPN-UK) is a voluntary, not-for-profit professional network. We decide how your information is used, so we are the “controller” of it. You can contact us about your data at ihpnuk@outlook.com.

What we collect

When you apply to join, we ask only for what we need:

  • your name and email address;
  • your mobile number, so we can add you to our members’ WhatsApp group, which is how the network keeps in touch;
  • the UK nation you live in, and optionally your town or city;
  • your profession and career stage;
  • confirmation that you meet our eligibility criteria, which includes having at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity;
  • optionally, what you would like from the network and how you heard about us.

Information about ethnic origin is “special category” data under UK data protection law, so we ask for your explicit consent before collecting it. We use it to check eligibility. Being in our members’ WhatsApp group also shows other members that you are of Igbo heritage, as explained below.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To assess your application and manage your membership: your consent, and your explicit consent for your Igbo heritage (UK GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)).
  • To add you to our members’ WhatsApp group: the group is how IHPN-UK runs, so every member is added to it. Because being in the group shows other members your Igbo heritage, we rely on the explicit consent you give on the application form (UK GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)).
  • To send you news and event updates by email: only if you tick the box to agree. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We never sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it is kept

Applications are collected using Cognito Forms and kept in IHPN-UK’s Cognito Forms account. Cognito Forms processes the data for us under its own privacy and security terms, and may store it outside the UK with legal safeguards in place. Only the Founder and President, or a team member they authorise, can see applications.

What you share in our members’ WhatsApp group is kept on WhatsApp, not in Cognito Forms. See Our members’ WhatsApp group below.

How long we keep it

  • If your application is not approved, we delete it within 3 months.
  • If you become a member, we keep your details while you are a member and delete them within 12 months of you leaving.
  • When your membership ends, we remove you from our members’ WhatsApp group, and your mobile number is deleted along with your other details.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and you can object to how we use your data. To do any of these, email ihpnuk@outlook.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Our members’ WhatsApp group

IHPN-UK runs through a members’ WhatsApp group. When your application is approved, we add you to it using the mobile number you gave us.

  • Who can see you: everyone in the group can see your phone number, your WhatsApp name and profile picture, and anything you post. You can limit who sees your profile picture in WhatsApp’s privacy settings.
  • Your heritage: because this is a group for Igbo healthcare professionals, being in it shows other members, and WhatsApp, that you are of Igbo heritage. You agree to this when you tick the consent box on the application form.
  • Introducing yourself: when you join, we invite you to say hello to the group. This is optional. Share only what you are happy for other members to see, for example your first name, your profession and the part of the UK you are in.
  • Keeping it safe: please never share patient details or anyone else’s personal information. Please don’t copy or share other members’ details or messages outside the group without their permission.
  • Leaving: you can leave the group at any time. Because the group is how we run the network, leaving it means leaving IHPN-UK, and we then delete your details as set out above. Messages you have already posted stay visible to the group unless you delete them first.

WhatsApp is run by Meta and may store data outside the UK. WhatsApp’s UK privacy policy (opens in a new tab) also applies to the group.

Emailing us

Our email account is provided by Microsoft Outlook, and only the Founder and President, or a team member they authorise, can read it.

  • Questions and enquiries: we use your name, email address and message only to reply to you (our legitimate interest in answering you). We delete enquiry emails within 12 months.
  • Registering interest in an event, such as our mental health talk: we use your email address only to send you details of that event (your consent). We delete the list within 3 months after the event.
  • Sharing your story: we use what you send to discuss a possible feature with you, and we publish it only with your written consent. You can withdraw consent at any time and we will remove it from future editions and from our website. If we don’t publish it, we delete it within 12 months.

Please don’t include patient details or health information in emails to us.

Contacting us on TikTok

If you message us on TikTok, TikTok’s own privacy policy also applies to that message. Please don’t send sensitive details by direct message; use the online form or email instead.

Comments you leave on our TikTok videos are public, and anyone can see them. We may reply to a comment or, with your permission, quote it in our content. Please don’t include patient details, health information or your employer’s name in comments. TikTok’s privacy policy applies to everything on TikTok.
