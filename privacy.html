Your data

Privacy notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information when you apply to join IHPN-UK or get in touch.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

The Igbo Healthcare Professionals Network UK (IHPN-UK) is a voluntary, not-for-profit professional network. We decide how your information is used, so we are the “controller” of it. You can contact us about your data at ihpnuk@outlook.com.

What we collect

When you apply to join, we ask only for what we need:

  • your name and email address;
  • your phone number, only if you would like to join our members’ WhatsApp group;
  • the UK nation you live in, and optionally your town or city;
  • your profession and career stage;
  • confirmation that you meet our eligibility criteria, which includes having at least one parent of Igbo ethnicity;
  • optionally, what you would like from the network and how you heard about us.

Information about ethnic origin is “special category” data under UK data protection law, so we ask for your explicit consent before collecting it and we only use it to check eligibility.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To assess your application and manage your membership: your consent, and your explicit consent for your Igbo heritage (UK GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)).
  • To send you news and event updates by email: only if you tick the box to agree. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We never sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it is kept

Applications are collected using Cognito Forms and kept in IHPN-UK’s Cognito Forms account. Cognito Forms processes the data for us under its own privacy and security terms, and may store it outside the UK with legal safeguards in place. Only the Founder and President, or a team member they authorise, can see applications.

How long we keep it

  • If your application is not approved, we delete it within 3 months.
  • If you become a member, we keep your details while you are a member and delete them within 12 months of you leaving.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and you can object to how we use your data. To do any of these, email ihpnuk@outlook.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Contacting us on TikTok

If you message us on TikTok, TikTok’s own privacy policy also applies to that message. Please don’t send sensitive details by direct message; use the online form or email instead.
